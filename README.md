## What it does

Sales Call Analyzer transcribes and scores every call: talk ratios, discovery depth, objection handling, and next-step discipline — then turns patterns into coaching moments for each rep.

## Key features

- **Call scorecards** — structured scoring per call against your methodology.
- **Objection library** — every objection tagged with winning responses.
- **Coaching moments** — timestamped clips worth reviewing, auto-selected.
- **Deal signals** — competitor mentions, budget hints, and risk flags to CRM.
- **Team benchmarks** — what top reps do differently, quantified.

## Who it's for

Sales leaders who can't listen to every call but want every rep coached.
